About FleriLab

FleriLab is rooted in the heart of Haiti, inspired by the resilience and potential of our people and land. Our mission is to support sustainable agriculture and scientific innovation by building laboratories and greenhouses tailored to Haiti's unique environment and needs.

We work hand-in-hand with local communities to address challenges like soil quality, crop resilience, and food security. Through education, research, and collaboration, FleriLab empowers the Haitian people to take the lead in cultivating solutions that strengthen their agricultural systems and sustain their livelihoods.

FleriLab is more than a laboratory! It’s a commitment to Haiti’s future. By combining traditional knowledge with advanced scientific practices, we aim to grow not only crops but also opportunities, self-reliance, and hope for generations to come.

Together, we are helping Haiti bloom! One step, one seed, and one innovation at a time. Ayiti Fleri!

Meet Our Founder

Meet Dashiell Desravines

Dashiell Desravines is a dedicated scientist with a deep passion for plants and their transformative potential. Currently pursuing his Master’s degree in Biology at the University of Central Florida, Dashiell combines his academic expertise with a commitment to fostering sustainable agricultural solutions. His vision for FleriLab is to empower communities, starting with Haiti, by advancing plant science, promoting food security, and nurturing growth both in the fields and within the people it serves.
